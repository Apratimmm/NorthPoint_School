North Point English Boarding School was established in 1998 with just forty-one students, six teachers, and one primary school building. Since then, we have grown into a thriving school with more than nine hundred students.

Our school has classrooms, science and computer laboratories, a library, canteen, and sports grounds all within one safe and welcoming campus. This allows our students to learn, play, and take part in different activities in one place.

While we are proud of our academic achievements and examination results, we believe education is about more than marks. We want our students to become respectful, responsible, kind, and confident individuals who care about the people and community around them.

OUR VISION

To be a school where curiosity is respected, character is nurtured with purpose, and every child is encouraged to discover their potential and leave ready to lead the life ahead of them. We envision a learning community where students are encouraged to ask questions, think independently, and approach the world with an open and curious mind. We want education to extend beyond textbooks and examinations, helping students develop integrity, resilience, empathy, and a strong sense of responsibility. We want every student to leave with the knowledge to move forward, the character to stand strong, and the curiosity to keep learning.

OUR MISSION

To teach simply, thoroughly, and with purpose — creating an environment where every student is known by name, understood as an individual, and given the support they need to grow. Our mission is to make learning clear, purposeful, and lasting — while helping every student grow into a confident, capable, and responsible individual.

OUR GOAL

To ensure that every student who leaves North Point is well prepared for the next stage of their education, confident in their abilities, physically active, and grounded in the values and culture they come from. We aim to nurture students who think independently, communicate respectfully, work well with others, and approach challenges with curiosity and determination. Beyond academic success, we want every student to develop the habits, character, and confidence needed to make thoughtful choices and contribute positively to their family, community, and wider society. Our goal is simple: to help students become capable learners today and curious, responsible individuals for life. long after the school bell.
