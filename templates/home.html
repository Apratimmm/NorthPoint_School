Since its humble beginning in Itahari, North Point English Boarding School has grown to become a respected institution for school education, built through the hard work, dedication, and vision of its founders, teachers, and staff.


Today, North Point provides quality education from the primary level through high school, offering students a supportive environment where they can develop academically, socially, and personally. Our dedicated teachers and staff work together to provide students with the knowledge, skills, and values they need to succeed in their future.


We are proud of the achievements of our students in academics and extracurricular activities, as well as the trust and support we have received from parents and the wider community over the years.


We warmly welcome you to explore our website and learn more about North Point English Boarding School.
